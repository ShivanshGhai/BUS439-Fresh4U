# Labour Planner app (streamlit_app.py)

Scope: the whole Streamlit app, every tab. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: Fresh4U growers/managers (Oct 27 visit) and the BUS 439 class (Nov 3, projector), equally. Brand: logo, green #00972D and tomato red stay; red only for failed checks.
Complaints to fix (Shivansh): walls of text, numbers don't stand out, feels generic, charts are flat.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The year is the interface. A grower's 52-week season wall planner is the app's spine: every tab answers its question against where it sits in the season. Refuses the category default of equal KPI tiles over paragraph callouts.

OWN-WORLD: Printed season planner. White paper ground, ink-black week grid with heavy rules, crop phases as saturated flat bands (vine green, truss blue, packing ochre, strawberry plum, clean-out rose), week numbers and big figures in condensed Archivo, body in Archivo normal width. Deep greenhouse-green header band carries the logo on a white plate. Amber marks things to check, tomato red only failed checks.

STORY: The visitor sees the year's shape, then the answer to the tab's question as one big number in a sentence, then the evidence. Method notes stay in expanders.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green header band (logo plate, "Labour Planner", data-source chip). Beneath it, full width, the Season strip: 52 week columns, height = people needed, coloured by the leading activity, crew line, month labels, a black marker over the weeks the open tab is about. Then tabs, then the answer line: lead number at ~3.5rem condensed inside a sentence, a ruled ledger of 3–5 supporting figures.

FORM: Season wall planner, candidate 1 of my ordered list (user chose Impeccable's pick over the assigned bulletin). seed:86ad62556467ba14 (degraded roll, no challengers).

Signature: the Season strip, drawn per tab with that tab's weeks marked (pay period, season, busiest week).
